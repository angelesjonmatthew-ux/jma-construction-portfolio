Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026

This website is a portfolio for my work as a civil engineer and construction estimator. To understand how many people visit and where they come from, I use two analytics tools.

What is collected

  • Approximate location (country and city), based on your IP address
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • Pages viewed, time on site, and how you arrived (for example, search or a link)

What is not collected

I do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal details through analytics. I cannot identify you personally from this data.

Tools used

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: counts visits and page views without cookies.
  • Google Analytics: uses cookies to measure visits and visitor behavior. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Your choices

You can block analytics by using an ad blocker, enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser, or clearing or blocking cookies in your browser settings.
