01 / ABOUT

Construction experience. Estimating focus.

Hi, I’m Jon Matthew.

As a Civil Engineer, I provide remote quantity takeoffs and cost estimation for global contractors and subcontractors. I bridge field experience with digital precision, turning architectural and engineering plans into clear, accurate Bills of Quantities (BOQs) to help teams submit stronger, faster bids.

Using Bluebeam Revu, PlanSwift, and Excel, I deliver detailed material and labor estimates across landscaping, civil works, finishes, and earthworks. My focus is simple: eliminate cost variations, prevent material shortages, and give you reliable numbers you can trust.

Looking for a dependable estimating partner? Let’s connect.
